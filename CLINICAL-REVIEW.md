# Clinical review checklist

Bloom is educational, not medical advice. Please have a doctor or nurse (ideally a Nigerian-trained clinician) check the high-stakes claims below, and adjust wording to local guidelines.

## Intimate and sexual health (modules 3, 10)
- [ ] No douching; wash the outside only; passing urine after sex.
- [ ] Discharge normal vs. red-flag descriptions.
- [ ] Condom use, oil-based lubricants and latex, never doubling up.
- [ ] STI testing triggers; many STIs are symptomless.
- [ ] HIV PEP "as soon as possible, within 72 hours"; PrEP mention.
- [ ] HPV and hepatitis B vaccine wording; cervical screening ("ask your doctor when to start and how often").
- [ ] Sexual assault guidance, including advice to avoid washing if considering reporting, and the Mirabel Centre mention (LASUTH, Ikeja, Lagos; free medical, counselling, legal support). Confirm the details are still current.

## Periods (module 4)
- [ ] Pad/tampon/cup change times, toxic shock syndrome warnings, cup sterilising.
- [ ] Heavy bleeding and pain red flags.

## Skin, hair, hands, feet, mouth (modules 5, 6)
- [ ] Product-safety statements (bleaching/lightening products, hair relaxers, fungal infection advice).
- [ ] Diabetic foot-care advice.

## Water and home (module 9)
- [ ] Boil 1 minute at a rolling boil; chlorine tablet guidance; SODIS about 6 hours in bright sun.
- [ ] Tank cleaning frequency (twice a year).
- [ ] Never mix bleach with ammonia or acids; no disinfectant in drinking water.
- [ ] Food storage "about 2 hours in the heat".

## Pregnancy, postpartum, contraception (module 11)
- [ ] Emergency contraception windows (72 hours pill; up to 5 days for some types and copper IUD).
- [ ] Warning signs in pregnancy and after birth; lochia duration (4 to 6 weeks).
- [ ] Pads rather than tampons or cups until cleared.
- [ ] Folic acid mention; cord care; baby-blues vs. postpartum depression wording.

## Later life (module 12)
- [ ] Any bleeding after menopause needs checking.
- [ ] Check-up list frequencies are deliberately vague; confirm they suit local practice.

## Products section
- [ ] Safety redirects in the product finder (symptoms go to a clinician).
- [ ] Example brands available in Nigeria; price bands.

## Find care and pocket guide
- [ ] Emergency line wording (112 national number, rollout ongoing; Lagos 767).
- [ ] Lagos DSVA toll-free 0800-333-333 and USSD *6820# (MTN); Mirabel Centre location and services. Re-verify before each release.
- [ ] "Who to see" descriptions of PHCs, general/teaching hospitals and pharmacies match what is realistically offered locally.
- [ ] Pocket guide one-liners (pad/tampon change times, EC and PEP timing, see-a-doctor list).
